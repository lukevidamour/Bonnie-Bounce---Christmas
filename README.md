# Bonnie Bounce — Christmas

A Christmas edition of Bonnie Bounce, the pixel-art platformer Luke made
with Claude for his son Bonnie. It's private, made for the family. Eleven
levels of Christmas Eve in Guernsey, for Bonnie and his cousins Beau and
Winnie, and anyone in the family can play.

> It's Christmas Eve in Guernsey. Father Christmas is on his last
> practice flight over St Peter Port when Grabby Gus, the greediest gull
> in Guernsey, spots the sleigh's shiny golden bell. SQUAWK! The reindeer
> take fright, the sleigh crash-lands on the quay, the eight reindeer
> scatter all over the island, and Gus flies off with the bell.
> "Ho ho… oh no!" Find the reindeer, win back the bell from Gus on Sark,
> and fly the sleigh over the rooftops before Christmas morning.

It's its own game with its own link. The original Bonnie Bounce, with its
fifty-five levels, is in `../Bonnie Bounce/`, and the two never share a
save (see *Two editions, one address* below). `CHRISTMAS-BRIEF.md` is the
brief this edition was built from, with Luke's answers and the plan he
approved.

## Playing it

Double-click `index.html` and it opens in a browser. There's nothing to
install and it doesn't need the internet: it works straight from the
folder.

On an iPad or phone, open its link (or host the folder anywhere static).
**Add to Home Screen** gives it the whole screen, with Bonnie in a Santa
hat as the icon.

Play it **sideways**. Three big see-through buttons sit over the game:
◀ ▶ under your left thumb and the big jump button under your right (the
whole right half of the screen jumps, and holding jumps higher). There's
no run button: hold a direction and you speed up. With the Super Ball a
throw button appears beside JUMP. The paper button top right pauses;
sound is in the pause menu. Menus, the map and the sticker book are
tapped. Held upright it still plays, with a small note suggesting
turning sideways.

The first tap on the title starts the music (browsers keep a page silent
until it's tapped); the next one plays.

**It keeps itself up to date.** Every time it opens it asks for the newest
files first; if it's been sitting open on a home screen and a new version
has been published, it reloads itself on the title or the map, never in
the middle of a level. It keeps a copy too, so once opened it plays with
no internet.

### Controls

| | Keyboard | Touch | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|---|
| Move | ← → (or A / D) | ◀ ▶ | D-pad / stick |
| Jump (hold for higher) | Space, Z or ↑ | JUMP (or anywhere on the right) | bottom / right button |
| Run | ⌘ Command, Option, Shift or X | hold ◀ or ▶ | left / top button |
| Throw a ball | the run key | the throw button | left / top button |
| Duck (when big) | ↓ | — | down |
| Pause · start | Enter or Esc | the paper pause button | Start |
| Sound on/off | M | in the pause menu | — |
| Full screen | F | — | — |

Every prompt shows the controls you're actually using, and switches the
moment you do. In the sea, tap jump to swim. Flying the sleigh, hold jump
to climb and let go to glide.

**Getting about the map.** Tap a level to walk there, or tap **GO** (under
BOOK, top left) for **WHERE TO?**: every level reached in a row, a gold
dot on the finished ones. Tap one and you're there.

## Who's playing

Seven of the family. A new game starts by asking **WHO'S PLAYING?**, so
Beau and Winnie begin as themselves, not as Bonnie. Tap yourself: you
cheer, sparkles burst, HELLO BEAU!, and a moment later the story starts
with you in it. Tapped the wrong one? Tap the right one before it goes,
or tap the same one again to go at once. On a keyboard it's ◀ ▶, then OK.
You can swap any time on the same page in the sticker book:

- **Bonnie**, 6
- **Beau**, 8, and **Winnie**, 5, his cousins
- **Aunty Lucy and Uncle Luke**, Bonnie's mum and dad
- **Aunty Grace and Uncle Ollie**, Beau and Winnie's mum and dad

Everyone plays exactly the same, with the same jump and the same hitbox.
They're drawn from Luke's photos on Bonnie's own puppet, so every outfit
and hat fits everyone.

- **Beau:** long wavy golden hair and his blue tracksuit. Leave him
  standing and he does keepy-ups with a snowball.
- **Winnie:** light-blond hair parted in the middle and his black bomber
  jacket. He cheers with a double thumbs up.
- **Uncle Ollie:** honey-gold hair, a ginger beard and a sage-green
  jumper.
- **Aunty Grace:** dark curls and her red dress. She twirls when she
  cheers.

Whoever isn't playing turns up all over the place: cheering at the flags,
answering the knock-knock doors in the Town, opening the cottage door at
the end of each level, and together round the tree at the end.

Everything the game says uses the names children use, and it's the
same whoever's playing, so it's as much Beau and Winnie's game as
Bonnie's: the children by name, and the grown-ups "AUNTY LUCY", "UNCLE
LUKE", "AUNTY GRACE" and "UNCLE OLLIE". Father Christmas calls everyone
by their first name.

## The game

Eleven levels, one Christmas Eve, from the harbour in the morning to the
rooftops at midnight. Each is about 440 tiles long, with three golden
medals, Bear and Prince to find, advent-calendar doors, a secret way
through something (a secret wall at each end, so it's a tunnel, never a
dead end) and a set piece of its own. Each ends at a cosy granite cottage
where someone from the family opens the door with a treat (a mince pie,
gâche, hot chocolate, a candy cane, a gingerbread man, a pudding). Beside
it is whatever was lost there, up to something silly.

| | Level | What's in it | Found at the end |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Harbour Lights | St Peter Port harbour in the morning: Father Christmas by his crashed sleigh, the first cracker, bobbing boats, the market's awnings, Jingle Bells laid into the quay | Dasher, dizzy, wrapped in tinsel |
| 2 | Frosty Morning | Candie Gardens: a frozen pond, the glasshouses, a snowdrift with a medal in it, the giant snow globe (shake it and the first snow falls), the sledge down the top lawn | Prancer, skating in wobbly circles |
| 3 | The Noon-Day Gun | Castle Cornet: puddings rolling down the ramparts, the noon-day gun that fires you over the keep (or the keep's secret passage), the battlements, the gardens | Dancer, dancing |
| 4 | The Green Lanes | hedge-veg stalls with honesty boxes, Guernsey cows to ride (hop along the cows' choir and they moo Jingle Bells), puddles, the blue postbox's whoosh of letters up into the sky | Vixen, among the cows, going MOO |
| 5 | Saumarez Park | everyone on bikes: trees and the lake bridge strung with lights that come on as you ride, the playground, the hedge maze | Father Christmas's hat, on a duck |
| 6 | Sunset Bays | Cobo and Vazon at sunset: a sledge down the dunes, crabs in Santa hats, seals to bounce on, the tide going out to show a path through the rock pools | Cupid, buried to his antlers in a sandcastle |
| 7 | The Cup and Saucer | Fort Grey at dusk: a shipwreck to climb, and on its deck **Grabby Gus's nest**, heaped with everything shiny he's pinched (your paper crown back, if a gull took it) and the clue that he's off to Sark; rigging to swing on, treasure rooms, the fort to climb | Donner, stuck on the cottage roof |
| 8 | Town Lights | St Peter Port's steep streets as it gets dark: lamps coming on as you pass, knock-knock doors, fairy-light bridges, chimney smoke, and the big red button that switches on the whole Town | Comet, tangled in fairy lights |
| 9 | Shell Beach | the ferry to Herm, the dunes, the quarry, Shell Beach by moonlight, seals | Blitzen, in a deckchair, in sunglasses |
| 10 | Sark by Starlight | the boat, the horse and cart, shooting stars, the silver mine, La Coupée, and **Grabby Gus**: he dives and crash-lands, so stomp him while he's dizzy. Three stomps and he's sorry, and the golden bell is back | (the bell) |
| 11 | Over the Rooftops | Christmas Eve at midnight: fly Father Christmas's sleigh, dropping a present down every chimney, then across the roofs on foot, and the sleigh again home | home |

**The chase.** Grabby Gus is never far away. Once in every level from 1
to 9 he swoops in from behind with the golden bell, low over your head,
shouting something cheeky ("CAN'T CATCH ME!", "MOO TO YOU!", "NO BIKES IN
THE SKY!"), and is off ahead and away. He never hurts anyone: jump and
tag him as he goes over and he drops some of his shiny loot (OI! MY
SHINIES!). In level 7 he warns you to keep out of his nest, and it's right
there on the wreck's deck. In level 9 it's "SEE YOU ON SARK!". On the map
he circles the island with the bell, and once his nest is found he's off
east, towards Sark.

After level 11 comes **Christmas morning**: the whole family round the
tree, Bear and Prince in jumpers, Gus in a paper crown with a bag of
chips, and Father Christmas flying over with all eight reindeer. Then the
credits, then the **Boxing Day dip**. After that the map is Christmas
morning too, for going back to find what's still hidden.

The reindeer are the eight from the 1823 poem (Dasher, Dancer, Prancer,
Vixen, Comet, Cupid, Donner, Blitzen). No Rudolph, and no red nose.

### The Christmas cast

The baddies are a bit silly, never scary:

- **sprouts** hop about crossly and go PFFT when stomped
- **snowmen** have colds: AH… AH… ACHOO! and the carrot nose shoots off
- **Grabby Gus's gulls** swoop at you, and pinch your paper crown if you're wearing one
- **crabs** wear Santa hats
- **Christmas puddings** roll down hills at you (land on one: BOING)

The friends:

- **robins** flutter off as you come near
- **Guernsey cows** you can stand on and ride
- **seals** in the sea bounce you up high
- **Father Christmas**

### Things to find, and things to pull

- **Three golden medals** in every level.
- **Bear**, the family's grey cat, and **Prince**, Beau and Winnie's
  black-and-white cat, hide in every level. Touch one and they follow you
  home, walking your own path a moment behind (Prince behind Bear). Bring
  them home and their faces light up on that level's tag on the map.
- **Advent-calendar doors**, 24 across the eleven levels, mostly in
  secret places. Each one found opens that door in the sticker book's
  advent calendar.
- **Christmas crackers.** Touch one and BANG: confetti, a joke, a
  surprise (stars, a heart, a cupcake), and a **paper crown** on your
  head. While you wear it, a bump takes the crown instead of your heart.
  A gull would like it for himself.
- **Secret walls** that look solid but walk straight through, with a room
  behind. **Balloons** float you up into the sky. **Snowdrifts** are for
  jumping in (one or two hide a medal).

**Hearts:** you have one. A bump while small, or a fall into the sea or
a pit, and you start again from the flag. Lives are unlimited. When
you're big (a cupcake), a bump shrinks you first; a paper crown goes
before that. A **heart** from a present gives a second heart for the rest
of the level.

**Power-ups** come out of the presents: the **cupcake** (big, can smash
bricks), the **Super Ball** (throw bouncing balls), the **rainbow lolly**
(a few seconds of invincibility) and the **heart**.

**Grown-ups** (title menu or pause menu, hold to open) can record their
own "Yay!", "Wheee!" and "Well done!", played at flags, on springs and
when a level's clear. The clips stay on that device, and they're this
edition's own (record them again here). **OPEN LEVEL n** opens the next
level without finishing the one before, one per tap.

## Two editions, one address

Both editions live on the same github.io address, which shares browser
storage, so everything this one keeps has its own name:

- the save is `bonnie-christmas-save`
- the mute switch is `bonnie-christmas-mute`
- the grown-ups' voices are `bonnie-christmas-voice-…`
- the service worker's cache is `bonnie-christmas`

To reset the save, clear the site's storage in the browser, or run
`localStorage.removeItem('bonnie-christmas-save')` in its console.

## For grown-ups who want to tinker

Everything is plain HTML and JavaScript, with no build step.

The game thinks in "game pixels" (8x8 tiles, a 144-pixel-tall view) and
draws each one as 2x2 screen pixels. Sprites are typed at full density,
built from shapes lit from the top left, with an outline in a deep shade
of their own colour. The players are a posed puppet (head, then jumper,
sleeves, hands, shorts, legs and shoes on joints), so a new pose is a few
numbers in `POSES` in `js/art.js`, and a new person is a head, a face and
a `look` (how their clothes differ).

| File | What's in it |
|---|---|
| `js/core.js` | screen scaling, the sprite painter, pixel font, input, the sound chip (the Christmas sounds too: PFFT, ACHOO, MOO, BANG, sleigh bells) |
| `js/art.js` | the players on Bonnie's puppet: Bonnie, Mum and Dad, Beau, Winnie, Uncle Ollie and Aunty Grace; their outfits' shapes (`kits.school`, `kits.kit`, `kits.bones`); tiles; the original's themes |
| `js/creatures.js` | the original's creatures, items and scenery (Bear, and Prince drawn on Bear) |
| `js/creatures6a.js` … `6c.js` | the original's Half Term art, kept for the engine features it draws (puddles, leaf piles, kites…) |
| `js/creaturesx.js` | the Christmas cast and set pieces: sprouts, snowmen, Gus and his gulls, crabs in hats, robins, cows, seals, the reindeer, Father Christmas, the sleigh, crackers, the snow globe, the noon-day gun, the veg stall, the postbox, the cottage, the treats, the advent doors, the Christmas tree |
| `js/family.js` | the grown-ups' tall cameos (Lucy, Luke, Ollie, Grace), Bonnie on his bike and anyone's bike |
| `js/backdrop.js` | the backdrop painting kit (and the original's backdrops) |
| `js/backdropx1.js` | worlds 1–6: their themes, backdrops and scenery props |
| `js/backdropx2.js` | worlds 7–11, the title's Town at dusk and Christmas morning |
| `js/light.js` | sunbeams, dusk and glowing lights |
| `js/map.js` | the map of Guernsey, Herm and Sark |
| `js/music.js` | the original's songs (some still used: the star, the clear jingle) |
| `js/musicx.js` | the Christmas soundtrack |
| `js/levels.js` | the level builder (its commands are explained at its top) |
| `js/levelsx.js` | the eleven levels |
| `js/ui.js` | the game's stationery: paper cards, ribbons, a Christmas-pudding pointer |
| `js/voice.js` | the grown-ups' recorded voices |
| `js/game.js` | physics, the players, everything they meet (the Christmas set pieces under CHRISTMAS EVE IN GUERNSEY), the screens and the save |
| `js/touch.js` | touch play |
| `sw.js`, `js/fresh.js` | always the newest version, and offline play |
| `manifest.webmanifest`, `icons/` | home-screen install; the icons are drawn by `tools/icon.html` |

Handy while editing:

- `index.html?level=7` jumps straight into level 7. `index.html?debug`
  opens every level on the map. `index.html?touch` plays as a touch
  screen does, to check the iPad layout on a desktop.
- `tools/sheet.html` shows every sprite, magnified (`?z=8` to zoom;
  `?only=xmas` the Christmas things; `?only=cousins` the new players).
- `tools/look.html?who=beau,winnie&k=idle,cheer,school,kit` shows chosen
  players' frames (and outfits) up close; `?e=sprout,gus` a creature's.
- `tools/backdrops.html?theme=candie,sark` shows a world's backdrop.
- `tools/map.html` draws the map in any state.
- `tools/overview.html?level=all` draws every level flat.
- `tools/playtest.html?level=all` sends a robot through every level to
  prove each can be finished. It plays with one heart, so a level it
  finishes can be finished without a single hit. It also restarts from
  every flag to check you can walk off it. Add `&medals=1` to go after
  each medal, `&bear=1` to find Bear, `&prince=1` to find Prince, and
  `&stuck=1` to hunt for places you can get into but not out of.

Levels are written with short commands. A few examples:

- `L.ground(0, 56)` and `L.row(16, 11, 'BPB?B')` for ground and blocks
- `L.medal(x, row)`, `L.secret(x0, y0, x1, y1)` and `L.balloons(x, row)`
- `L.e('cracker', x, 16)` and `L.e('cow', x, 16, { range: 6 })` for the
  Christmas set pieces
- `L.bear(x, row)` and `L.e('prince', x, row)`

They're explained at the top of `js/levels.js`; the Christmas set pieces
are explained in `js/game.js` where they're defined.

### Making a trailer

`tools/reel/` films a 16:9 trailer straight from the game (no HUD, no
words, only play), cut on the music's bars. Its Christmas scenes are a
first draft and nothing's been filmed: per the brief, the trailer comes
later and has only what Luke approves. See the top of
`tools/reel/reel.js` for how it works.

### First-play review (29 Oct)

A new player's first few minutes, played at 8 screens with `?touch`:
phones held sideways (667×375, 844×390, 932×430), a phone upright
(390×844), and iPads (1024×768, 1180×820, 820×1180 upright). That's every
game width from 160 to 312. To repeat it, load `index.html?touch`, add
`tools/qa/firstplay.js` and run `FP.run('844x390')`. It saves 12 moments
to `tools/reel/out/` (served by `tools/reel/serve.js`), and
`python3 tools/qa/sheet.py 844x390` tiles them into one sheet.

What it found, and what changed:

- **Level 1's boats** worked but looked like plank jetties floating in
  the air, lost against the harbour behind. They're now little painted
  boats (red, blue and green) sitting in the water, each with a holly
  wreath on the bow and Christmas lights up the mast. Level 7's boat too.
- **The title:** TAP TO START touched the CHRISTMAS ribbon, and the menu
  covered its lower edge. The ribbon moved up. On an iPad held sideways
  the trampoline also overlapped the ribbon's tail; it's now placed from
  the ribbon's real width.
- **The sticker book's WHO'S PLAYING? page:** the pudding cursor sat on
  the "TAP WHO YOU WANT TO BE" line, and the ribbon's tails clipped it.
  The cursor now points from beside whoever's chosen.
- **Narrow screens:** the longest cracker jokes ran off the screen on
  every iPad, and the story's captions ran off a phone held upright.
  Jokes and captions now break onto two even lines, and the story's big
  words step down to a size that fits. Floating words said near the edge
  of the screen are nudged onto it.
- **Names:** Lucy and Luke are Aunty Lucy and Uncle Luke whoever is
  playing, as Grace and Ollie are Aunty Grace and Uncle Ollie, so it's as
  much Beau and Winnie's game as Bonnie's.
- **Starting as yourself:** a new game asks WHO'S PLAYING? before the
  story (above). The story now ignores taps for its first moment, so an
  excited second tap on yourself doesn't skip it.
- **The title:** Bonnie, Beau and Winnie are all on it, big: whoever's
  playing bounces on the trampoline, the other two cheer on the left each
  time they land. A soft night-blue veil over the town lets the name and
  the children stand out, and the parade along the bottom is a few
  friendly faces (a reindeer, Bear, Prince, the robin, a snowman).
- **Apostrophes:** the game's pixel font has no curly apostrophe or
  accents, so IT'S SNOWING!, THE TIDE'S GOING OUT!, SORRY! HERE'S YOUR
  BELL!, FATHER CHRISTMAS'S HAT!, A SLICE OF GÂCHE! and one joke showed a
  "?" in them. The text drawing now uses the plain letters.

The responsive audit (`tools/qa/audit.py`, 23 sizes from 280×653 to
1920×1080) passes with nothing to fix. Its "loaded" check used to need
40 characters of page text, which a game drawn on a canvas doesn't have,
so it now also accepts a visible canvas.
